Intended use, method and limits

Model card

Intended use

Exploring how a treatment assumption changes a growth forecast for a single patient's tumour, and showing what the assumptions are. It is a research demo for a hackathon audience.

Not for choosing or changing treatment, giving a prognosis, or any decision about a person.

What is simulated

A reaction-diffusion model: cells proliferate at rate ρ, spread with diffusion D, and are lost at rate δ. It is seeded from the patient's segmentation masks (core 0.95, enhancing 0.5, oedema 0.2; resection cavity excluded) and run forward from the last scan.

Tumour burden is the sum of density times voxel volume, in cell-equivalent mm³. MRI does not measure cell density, so the density colouring is a modelling choice.

Limits to keep in mind

  • Scenario curves are what the model does under assumed effects. They are not what would have happened to this patient.
  • Growth rate ρ is calibrated on a single interval per patient. Some fits do not converge; those are flagged and should not be read.
  • Density is a seed built from segmentation labels, not a measurement. The 3D view is a 32×32×32 display grid, so fine structure is lost.
  • Treatment records come from clinical sheets; where an end date was missing it was assumed, and the assumption is listed per patient.
  • Missing values are shown as "not available". They are never replaced by zero.
